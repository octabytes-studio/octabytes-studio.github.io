02 / The studio

Small by design.
Not by accident.

Make it feel good.
Then make it feel finished.

OctaBytes is an independent studio making mobile games and apps. We shape the idea, design the loop, draw the world, write the code and sweat the release — in-house.

Fewer hand-offs, shorter feedback loops, and the people who make the thing are the people you talk to. We're not trying to ship fifty apps. We'd rather make a handful of things that earn a place on your home screen — and keep it.

01

Feel is a feature.

Input latency, haptics, the sound a button makes. The details players feel before they can name them.

02

Ship the whole thing.

A game isn't finished at the last level. Store art, privacy, accessibility and the first update are part of the craft.

03

Small on purpose.

A tight team from sketch to ship. No hand-off maze, no layers between you and the people making it.

04 / How we work

From spark to
store page.

A simple shape for a complicated thing. You'll always know where we are and what's next.

  1. 01

    Shape

    Start with the feeling and the loop, not a sixty-page brief. We find the smallest version worth making.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    Get it into hands quickly. Test the fun before we polish the paint.

  3. 03

    Build

    Design, code, art, sound and all the unglamorous systems that hold a game together.

  4. 04

    Ship

    Store art, privacy, pricing and release — then the first updates that make it last.

One team. One thread. No hand-off maze.
